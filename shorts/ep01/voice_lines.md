# EP 1 — Voice lines (record on your phone)

## How to record
- **One take per character.** Read that character's lines in order, with a **clear 2-second pause of silence** between lines. Don't talk or breathe loudly in the gaps.
- Quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, no echo (record under a blanket or in a wardrobe). Do 1 test line first.
- Save as `ep01/voice/<character>.m4a` (or .wav / .mp3):
  `hamid`, `yahya`, `lady`, `salma`, `mizhor`, `father`
- If a take gets messy, record the lines as separate files named by line ID instead (e.g. `voice/03_yahya.m4a`). They override the take.
- **TIME** = where the line starts in the 60 s episode; **SLOT** = how long you have. If a line runs a little long, that's fine. If it's way over, speak faster.
- The TTS track (`make_voice.py`) is only a timing guide. Your voice replaces it.

Spelling is the subtitle spelling: `3` = ع, `7` = ح, `9` = ق, `kh` = خ, `gh` = غ.
