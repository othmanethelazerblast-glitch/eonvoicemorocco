## Consistency rules
1. Always attach the character's reference image **and** paste the TAG.
2. Never describe faces differently between shots; only change action, camera and light.
3. Keep clothing fixed per look (Hamid A/B above; Yahya coat only in 3A/3D).
4. No readable text, logos, or brand names anywhere (labels and the ID card are added as overlays in the edit).
